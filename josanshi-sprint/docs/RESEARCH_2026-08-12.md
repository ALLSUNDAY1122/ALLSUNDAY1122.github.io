## Copyright / reuse audit

MHLW's general website terms state that, unless otherwise noted or separately rights-labelled, MHLW content is available under PDL1.0. PDL1.0 permits reproduction, public transmission, translation/adaptation and commercial use, but requires source attribution and, for modified content, a statement that it was edited/processed. PDL1.0 also explicitly says that third-party rights may exist and that users must identify and clear those rights unless clearance is explicitly stated.

Sources:
- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/chosakuken/index.html
- https://www.digital.go.jp/resources/open_data/public_data_license_v1.0

### Product policy

1. Do not make verbatim official-question reproduction the default content strategy.
2. Build original questions and original explanations from the current MHLW question standard, statutes, MHLW notices/guidelines and other primary professional sources.
3. If an official question is ever reproduced, audit the individual item for special notices and third-party content, record the exact source and PDL basis, and add required attribution / modification notices.
4. Do not reuse official booklet photos, illustrations, charts or other third-party-looking material unless the individual rights basis is confirmed. Replace with original diagrams only when clinically necessary and separately rights-audited.
5. Each production question must hold `sourceURL`, `sourceCheckedAt`, `lawBaselineDate`, `contentVersion`, `rightsBasis` and similarity metadata required by the shared audit loop.

## Problem-bank plan

- Production target: 3 original mock sets modeled on the current confirmed 110-item examination = 330 original items total.
- Per mock: 75 general + 35 situation-setting, preserving 12 linked scenario cases.
- Product total: 225 general + 105 situation-setting questions, with 36 linked scenario cases.
- Situation-setting questions must retain `scenarioId`, `scenarioIndex` and `scenarioTotal`; they must not be flattened into unrelated short questions.
- The four official subjects are fixed.
- Per-subject generation quotas are not claimed as official. The R5 standard gives scope, and `data/question-blueprint.json` defines a product-design allocation for coverage only.
- No padding/paraphrase duplication is allowed.

## Product risks

- Market-size risk: high — annual direct candidate pool is only about 2,000.
- Monetization risk: high — strong free competitors exist.
- Medical-content accuracy risk: high — outdated pregnancy/newborn guidance can create harmful misinformation.
- Rights risk: medium/high — official booklet images and other third-party elements cannot be assumed to be PDL-cleared.
- Platform risk: low/medium — native SwiftUI + offline architecture minimizes web/runtime dependency.

## Mitigation

- Evidence-date every medical/legal question.
- Current-source revalidation before each release.
- Separate content audit from app code audit.
- Preserve scenario linkage so situation-setting problems are clinically coherent rather than padded fragments.
- No production question bank is marked PASS until count, duplication, answer, explanation, source, baseline date and rights gates all pass.
- Keep App Store identifiers unset until canonical values are confirmed.
